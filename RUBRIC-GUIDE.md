# Rubric guide

**Release:** v17
**Use:** briefs, portfolio items and capstones.

## Four dimensions

Score every artifact on four dimensions, each 1–4.

1. **Evidence and provenance** — the claim is tied to data, a source, a page, an accession or a reproducible run.
2. **Method and calculation** — the method is appropriate, the arithmetic is checkable, units and assumptions are explicit.
3. **Interpretation and uncertainty** — alternatives are considered; intervals, limitations and falsification tests are stated.
4. **Communication and reproducibility** — a stranger can follow or rerun it; figures and tables are readable; limitations are not buried.

## Level definitions

| Level | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **4 — Exemplary** | All four dimensions are explicit and traceable. The work survives an attempt to falsify it. A stranger can reproduce it from the stated files. |
| **3 — Pass** | All required deliverables are present. One dimension is thin but acknowledged. No unsupported central claim. |
| **2 — Revise** | A required deliverable is missing, a calculation is uncheckable, or a central claim is asserted without evidence. |
| **1 — Fail** | No primary evidence, the non-negotiable is absent, or the conclusion exceeds the data. |

## Scoring rules

- **Lowest dimension decides the overall score.** A 4 in communication does not rescue a 1 in evidence.
- A failed **non-negotiable** caps the overall score at 2, even if the writing is excellent.
- **Score 4 requires external review.** Score 3 requires course-reviewer sign-off.
- A portfolio item with no dated artifact scores 1, regardless of prose.
- A brief that recycles a published answer without the required primary data scores 1 on evidence.

## What “non-negotiable” means

Each brief has one. It is listed in `BRIEF-RUBRICS.csv`. Examples:

- Brief 1: the bias of linearisation must be shown numerically.
- Brief 3: the balance must not be closed by scaling a measurement.
- Brief 8: the coupling must be proved from conservation, not asserted.
- Brief 14: the techno-economic verdict must be willing to be unwelcome.

Missing the non-negotiable is a 2 or lower.

## Portfolio review cadence

- Review 1: week 18.
- Review 2: week 31.
- Review 3: week 46.
- Final: week 55.

At each review, score every artifact due so far. Do not wait until the end.

## Calibration

For every weekly exit ticket:

1. Before answering, state confidence 1–5.
2. Answer closed-book.
3. Score 0–3 against the outcome.
4. Record whether the answer was correct after review.
5. If confidence exceeds score by 2 or more, add the error to the error log and retest in 7 days.

The goal is not high confidence. The goal is confidence that tracks evidence.
