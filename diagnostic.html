Answer key and one-line explanations
  1. A — DNA → RNA → protein.
  2. B — promoter binds RNA polymerase.
  3. C — ribosome translates mRNA.
  4. A — plasmid is an extrachromosomal replicon.
  5. D — nonsense gives a premature stop.
  6. B — resistance is a selection marker.
  7. A — transformation is DNA uptake.
  8. C — knockout is loss of function.
  9. B — operon genes share a promoter.
  10. D — conjugation, transformation and transduction are HGT.
  11. A — Km is the half-saturation substrate concentration.
  12. C — NADH is an electron carrier.
  13. B — net 2 ATP per glucose.
  14. A — competitive inhibition raises apparent Km.
  15. C — electrons must balance.
