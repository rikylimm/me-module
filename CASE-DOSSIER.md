## 3. Open conflicts

### 3.1 Fungal identity
Most passages: *Aspergillus oryzae*.
One starter-preparation passage: *Aspergillus niger* (printed p. 46 / PDF p. 70).

Quotation from the starter preparation:

> “…medium Lactobacilus plantarum, Bacillus subtilis, dan **Aspergillus niger** masing-masing adalah MRS Broth, Nutrient Broth (NB), dan Potato Dextrose Broth (PDB).”

The next sentence returns to *Aspergillus oryzae*. No strain identifiers or independent identity check are given. **Status: unresolved.** Confirm from culture records or strain stocks.

### 3.2 Whiteness vs SNI
Table 4.8 sets whiteness minimum 87. Validation reports 83.5. The abstract and conclusion say the product meets SNI.

This is an open discrepancy. It may reflect the standard’s scope, a different whiteness metric, or a conclusion that outran the result. Do not resolve it without checking the original standard and the raw measurement. **Status: unresolved.**

### 3.3 Replacement interval
Abstract and methods:

> “…penggantian air sebesar 25% **setiap waktu tinggal fermentasi**.”

Residence time is 8 h. Validation procedure:

> “…penggantian air sebesar 25% yang dilakukan **setiap 4 jam**.” (printed p. 103 / PDF p. 127)

Both statements are in the source. A 25% exchange every 4 h is twice per 8-h residence; that may be intentional or a reporting inconsistency. **Status: unresolved.** Check the operating log.

### 3.4 Ratio notation
- Abstract and conclusion: **7:1 water-to-substrate**.
- Methods narrative: **1:7 substrate-to-water**. This is the reciprocal of 7:1 and is the same condition if the basis is stated.
- Methods table: headed *“Rasio air terhadap substrat”* but lists **1:7** and **1:12** — the header and values conflict.
- Table 4.1 Run 2: **1:8** — a separate run, not the selected validation.

The arithmetic is not the problem. The problems are the table label and which run is compared. **Status: unresolved label conflict.** State kg water per kg fresh cassava and do not silently normalize.

## 4. What the thesis measured

B. subtilis counts were unusable in several runs because *B. cereus* overgrew the MYP agar (printed p. 104 / PDF p. 128; see also printed p. 66 / PDF p. 90).

Measured: microbial counts (*L. plantarum*, *B. subtilis*; *A. oryzae* gravimetric), broth pH, cyanogenic compounds in broth and flour, flour yield, moisture, swelling power, whiteness.

Not measured: enzyme activity, sugar release, organic-acid production rates, oxygen demand, intracellular fluxes.

Therefore:
- starch hydrolysis supplying glucose is a hypothesis;
- *L. plantarum* acidifying the broth is supported by pH, but which acids and at what rates are not measured;
- a consortium member limiting conversion is a hypothesis unless the substrate/product balance identifies it;
- a process optimum is not a cellular metabolic optimum.

## 5. Audit questions

1. Which fungal species and strain were actually used? Resolve *A. oryzae* vs *A. niger*.
2. Was the SNI whiteness criterion applied to the same metric and product scope? Why does 83.5 pass?
3. Was 25% exchanged every 4 h or every 8 h? Which record shows it?
4. Which ratio basis was used in the operating log: water-to-substrate or substrate-to-water?
5. What measurements would distinguish the stated metabolic explanations from alternatives?
6. Which claims in the thesis are measurements, which are inferences, and which are literature claims?
