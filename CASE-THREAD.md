## Biodiesel touchpoints

The Reutealis esterification work is reaction engineering and optimisation, not cell metabolism.

- **Week 45/52:** catalyst, methanol ratio, temperature and optimisation transfer as process reasoning.
- **Week 49:** reactor and mass-transfer reasoning transfers to fermentation.
- **Do not transfer:** heterogeneous catalysis, FFA conversion, or response-surface results to intracellular fluxes.
