# Guided project rubric

## Dimensions

1. **Balance and units** — carbon, electron and charge close or the missing stream is named.
2. **Model honesty** — toy assumptions and alternatives are explicit.
3. **Design** — replication, randomisation, power and effect size are justified.
4. **Evidence** — isotope choice and MDV logic are correct; limits are stated.
5. **Defence** — claim, alternative and falsifier are clear.

## Levels

- **4** — all five dimensions explicit and traceable; a stranger can rerun the model; the defence survives a falsification attempt.
- **3** — all dimensions present; one is thin but acknowledged.
- **2** — a stage is missing, a balance is forced, or the model is presented as predictive without support.
- **1** — no data, no balance, or a central claim exceeds the evidence.

A score of 4 requires external review.
