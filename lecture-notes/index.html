Lecture notes

The distilled modules for every week: a framing paragraph, the core ideas, a worked anchor with its assumptions, a read-closely / skim / skip reading map, common misconceptions, a self-test, a prompt that transfers the idea to your fermentation experience, and a closed-book teach-back with a calculation and assumption check. All 64 figures are injected at the core idea each belongs to.

Phases 1-3: Molecular foundations, metabolism in depth, and quantitative physiology (weeks 1-18).

Distilled modules: the content you need, a worked anchor, a reading map that tells you what to read closely and what to skip, misconceptions, and a self-test. Written to replace cover-to-cover reading, not to replace reading.

On the numbers before you read any of them. Every number in these modules — ribosome counts, pool sizes, half-lives, turnover times — is written from working knowledge and is an order-of-magnitude figure. Before any of them enters a notebook, a figure or a proposal, check it against BioNumbers or the primary source and cite that, not these notes. Treat the modules as a map of what matters and a scaffold for reasoning; treat the textbooks and the databases as the authority for values.

Every worked anchor states the assumptions it rests on, and every figure built from simulated rather than measured values says so in its title. Treat a calculation here as a conditional inference, not a result.
