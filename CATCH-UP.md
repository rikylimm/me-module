# Catch-up modules

**Release:** v23
**Use:** after the entry diagnostic, before Week 1 if the diagnostic points here.

The point is not to reteach a degree. It is to supply the minimum vocabulary the first seven weeks assume.

## A. Molecular biology — 5 sessions

1. **Information flow.** DNA → RNA → protein. Replication, transcription, translation. Read Week 7 before starting.
2. **Genes and control.** Promoter, operator, ribosome-binding site, terminator. What a promoter does and does not determine.
3. **Proteins.** Amino acids, peptide bond, primary to quaternary structure, folding, denaturation.
4. **Mutation.** Missense, nonsense, frameshift, deletion. Predict the protein consequence before the phenotype.
5. **Self-check.** Explain why a strong promoter can still give low active protein. Name three post-transcriptional bottlenecks.

Links: `lecture-notes/week-07.html`, `lecture-notes/week-03.html`, `lecture-notes/week-04.html`.

## B. Genetics and microbial handling — 5 sessions

1. **Genomes and plasmids.** Chromosome, plasmid, copy number, selection marker.
2. **Replication and segregation.** What a plasmid must carry to be maintained.
3. **Gene transfer.** Transformation, conjugation, transduction. Which one is horizontal transfer?
4. **Selection and screening.** Antibiotic selection is not the same as a screen for activity.
5. **Self-check.** Design a selection for a plasmid that carries a gene you cannot assay.

Links: `lecture-notes/week-32.html`, `lecture-notes/week-33.html`, `lecture-notes/week-35.html`.

## C. Biochemistry and redox — 5 sessions

1. **Functional groups and weak interactions.** Hydrogen bonds, charge, hydrophobic effect.
2. **Enzyme kinetics.** Km, Vmax, kcat, catalytic efficiency. What Km does and does not mean.
3. **Central carbon metabolism.** Glycolysis, TCA, PPP; net ATP and NADH/NADPH.
4. **Redox.** NAD+/NADH, NADP+/NADPH, FAD/FADH2; balance electrons, not just carbons.
5. **Self-check.** For glucose to ethanol, balance carbon, ATP and NADH. State what changes if biomass is also produced.

Links: `lecture-notes/week-05.html`, `lecture-notes/week-08.html`, `lecture-notes/week-09.html`, `lecture-notes/week-13.html`.

## Rule

Do not start the 55-week sequence with a gap you can name. Do the diagnostic, do the relevant catch-up module, then begin Week 1. The core path in `CORE-PATH.csv` assumes this vocabulary.
